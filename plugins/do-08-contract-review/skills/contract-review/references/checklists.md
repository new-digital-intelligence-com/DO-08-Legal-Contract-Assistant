# Per-document-type checklists

A checklist is a list of things whose *absence* is invisible. A liability cap that is too low
announces itself when you read the clause; a missing data-export right announces nothing at all.
That is what these are for — and it is why "not present" is a finding, not a blank.

Use the checklist for the document type. Reviewing a settlement agreement against the SaaS
checklist produces confident nonsense, so where none of these fits, review from the risk taxonomy
and say which checklist you used.

## Every contract, regardless of type

- Parties named with full legal entity names, and the right entities — a subsidiary signing for a
  parent binds nobody else
- Effective date, term, and what happens at the end
- Which clauses survive termination, and whether that list is sensible
- Notice mechanics: addresses, method, when notice is deemed received
- Entire-agreement clause, and whether it wipes out an SOW or order form you rely on
- Order of precedence between the master agreement, order forms, SOWs and any linked online terms
- Anything incorporated by reference to a URL — those change without notice
- Signature blocks present and correct, or absent (a pre-signing alert)
- Blank fields, "TBD", "$____", bracketed placeholders
- Referenced exhibits and schedules that are not attached

## NDA

| Check | What matters |
|---|---|
| Direction | One-way or mutual? A one-way NDA where both sides will disclose is the wrong instrument |
| Definition of Confidential Information | Marked-only, or everything disclosed? Standard exceptions present — public, already known, independently developed, lawfully received |
| Term | 2 years short, 3–5 typical, indefinite for trade secrets only |
| Purpose limitation | Is use restricted to the stated purpose, or merely disclosure? |
| Permitted recipients | "Representatives" defined? Flow-down obligation? Liability for their breach? |
| Residuals | Unaided memory only, and mutual? A one-way residuals clause guts the NDA for the party disclosing more |
| Non-solicitation | Present, mutual, contact-based, general advertising carved out |
| Standstill | Relevant in M&A diligence; a bare NDA rarely needs one |
| Return and destruction | Certification required? Carve-out for backups and legal-hold copies |
| Compelled disclosure | Notice to the other side, and time to seek a protective order |
| Injunctive relief | Pre-agreed, bond waived — normal and usually mutual |
| No licence, no obligation | Disclosure grants no IP rights and no obligation to transact |

## SaaS, MSA, subscription

| Check | What matters |
|---|---|
| Liability cap | 12 months standard; check what the carve-outs do to it |
| Uptime and credits | A percentage with no remedy is not an SLA. Chronic-failure exit right? |
| Suspension | For non-payment after notice and cure, or immediate and discretionary? |
| Data ownership | Customer owns customer data, unambiguously |
| Data export | Format, window, cost, and deletion on request |
| Aggregated / derived data | Can they use your data to train or benchmark? Is it truly de-identified? |
| Price increases | Capped, with notice, on renewal only |
| Auto-renewal | Notice window to prevent |
| Termination | Mutual convenience right; cure period for cause; refund of prepaid fees |
| Subprocessors | Notice, objection right, flow-down |
| Security | Standard committed to, audit or certification evidence, incident notice window |
| Insurance | E&O, cyber, at sensible limits |
| Support | Response times that are commitments, not aspirations |
| Professional services | Are deliverables owned by you or licensed? |

## Data processing agreement

| Check | What matters |
|---|---|
| Roles | Controller / processor / joint controller, and is the characterisation right? |
| Processing scope | Subject matter, duration, nature, purpose, data categories, data subjects |
| Instructions | Processing only on documented instructions, with a lawfulness carve-out |
| Subprocessors | Authorisation model, notice, objection, flow-down of equivalent terms |
| International transfers | Mechanism named — SCCs, adequacy, UK addendum — and actually attached |
| Security measures | Specific, or a bare "appropriate technical and organisational measures"? |
| Incident notice | Window, content, and assistance with the controller's own obligations |
| Data-subject requests | Assistance obligation, timescale, cost |
| Audit | Right of audit, or reliance on a certification? |
| Deletion and return | On termination, with a documented exception for legal retention |

## Employment and consulting

| Check | What matters |
|---|---|
| Classification | Employee or contractor, and does the substance match the label? |
| IP assignment | Present-tense assignment, scope limited to work in the role, prior inventions schedule |
| Moral rights | Waived where the jurisdiction allows |
| Non-compete | Duration, geography, scope — and see jurisdictions.md; several US states void these |
| Non-solicit | Employees and customers; contact-based; carve-out for general advertising |
| Confidentiality | Survives; carve-out for whistleblowing and protected disclosures |
| Garden leave / notice | Length, pay during it, right to shorten |
| Termination | Cause defined narrowly, cure where sensible, severance triggers |
| Change of control | Acceleration, good-reason resignation |
| Compensation | Bonus discretionary or earned? Equity vesting, cliff, treatment on exit |
| Expenses and equipment | Who supplies, who owns |
| Governing law | Employee-protective mandatory law may override the choice |

## Payment and merchant

| Check | What matters |
|---|---|
| Reserve / holdback | Amount, trigger, release conditions, or "at our discretion"? |
| Chargeback liability | Where it lands, whether it is capped, fraud-protection scope |
| Network rules | Incorporated by reference? Are they even accessible to you? Non-negotiable |
| Auto-debit | Can they debit your account, on what notice, with what dispute route |
| Settlement timing | When funds land; what delays them |
| Volume commitments | Realistic; shortfall penalty |
| Suspension and termination | Immediate on risk grounds is common; check the funds-release consequence |
| Termination tail | How long obligations and reserves survive |
| PCI compliance | Who bears cost, who bears liability for a breach |
| Fee changes | Notice, and whether you may exit on a change |

## M&A and purchase agreements

| Check | What matters |
|---|---|
| Consideration | Cash, stock, earnout mix; when each is paid |
| Earnout mechanics | What is measured, who measures it, buyer's discretion over the business, acceleration on breach, audit rights |
| Working capital | Target, collar, true-up mechanism and dispute process |
| Escrow / holdback | Amount, duration, release schedule, sole-recourse or not |
| Rep survival | 12–18 months general, longer for fundamentals and tax |
| Indemnity | Cap, basket type (deductible vs tipping), de minimis, materiality scrape |
| Sandbagging | Pro-buyer, anti-sandbagging, or silent — and what the governing law does with silence |
| MAC definition | Carve-outs for market and industry conditions, and disproportionate-effect exception |
| Covenants | Interim operating restrictions, exclusivity, non-solicit |
| Non-compete on sellers | Duration, scope; enforceability differs from the employment context |
| Employment terms for founders | Is compensation part of deal value or separate? |
| Conditions to closing | Regulatory, consents, financing — and who bears the risk of failure |

## Finder and broker

| Check | What matters |
|---|---|
| Fee percentage | Specified, or left blank? |
| Fee base | What counts as deal value — assumed debt, earnout, employment compensation, retained equity? |
| "Covered buyer" | How broadly defined? Carve-out for pre-existing relationships? |
| Tail period | 12–18 months typical; perpetual is a red flag |
| Exclusivity | Exclusive or not; can you run your own process? |
| Minimum fee | Floor amount regardless of deal size |
| Joint representation | Do they act for both sides? Is the conflict waived in advance? |
| Escrow deduction | Paid automatically from proceeds before you see them? |
| Termination | Can you exit, and what survives if you do? |
| Broker-dealer status | Registered, where securities are involved? An unregistered finder can make the fee unenforceable |
